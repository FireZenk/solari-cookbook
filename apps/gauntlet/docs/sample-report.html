The consent banner cannot be reached with the keyboard

BANNER_KEYBOARD_UNREACHABLE

60 Tab presses from the top of the document never moved focus into the visible consent banner. A visitor who does not use a mouse cannot accept or refuse — which fails the keyboard requirement and, at the same time, means consent cannot be freely given by that visitor.
